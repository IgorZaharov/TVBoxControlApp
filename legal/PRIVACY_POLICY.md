# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tiny TVBox Remote Control mobile app and to the TVBox Remote Receiver companion app (together, "the app"). The app is published by <DEVELOPER_NAME> ("we", "us").

## Summary

The app does not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. The app talks only to devices on your local network and to devices that you connect with Bluetooth. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app.

## Data that stays on your device

To work, the app keeps some data on your phone or tablet. This data never leaves your device, except to go to the TV device that you control.

| Data | Why the app keeps it |
|---|---|
| Names and local network addresses of your TV devices | To show your devices and to connect again |
| Bluetooth addresses of devices that you paired | To connect again over Bluetooth |
| A security certificate and key, made by the app | To connect securely to your TV device after pairing |
| The certificate fingerprint of each paired TV device | To make sure that the app connects to the same device each time |
| A pairing token for the companion receiver app | To connect the mouse without a new code each time |
| Your preferences and your controller layout | To keep your settings |

The app does not include this data in device backups.

You can delete the data of one device with "Forget" in the Devices screen. To delete all data, uninstall the app.

## Data that goes to your TV device

When you use the app, it sends these items to the TV device that you selected, on your local network or over Bluetooth:

- The buttons that you press, the text that you type in the app keyboard, pointer movements, and game controller input.
- The name and model of your phone or tablet. The TV device shows this name in its list of connected remotes.

The app receives the volume level, the power state, and the keyboard state from the TV device, to show them in the app.

## Permissions

The app asks only for the permissions that it needs. You can refuse a permission. Then the function that needs it does not work, and the other functions continue to work.

- Local network: to find TV devices on your Wi-Fi network and to control them.
- Bluetooth: to find and connect to TV devices over Bluetooth, as a keyboard, mouse, game controller, and remote control.
- Location: some older operating system versions require the location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices. The app does not read, use, or keep your location.
- Motion sensors: to move the pointer on the TV when you move the phone, and to move the light reflections on the buttons. The app uses the sensor data on your device at that time, and does not keep it.
- Vibration: for a short tick when you press a button.

## Home screen widget

The widget uses the same data on your device as the app. It sends commands only to the device that you select in the widget.

## Security

The connection to the TV device over Wi-Fi is encrypted. You pair the app with a code that the TV shows, and the app checks the identity of the TV device each time it connects.

The connection to the companion receiver app is on your local network and is not encrypted. It carries only pointer movements and clicks. A 4-digit code that the TV shows controls which phones can connect.

## Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, children included.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reports, or tracking. The app store from which you got the app can collect data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that data, except anonymous totals that the store gives to all developers (for example, the number of downloads).
